mem-fusion — sharing personas + problems

Three sharing tiers. One common memory problem. Solved today by mem-fusion + the Slack connector.

Doc target: v0.7 launch readiness · Audience: mem-fusion developers + stakeholders · Status: draft for architect + marketing review

The common long-term memory problem

"My AI assistant treats every conversation as fresh. Decisions I made last week, last month, or with a teammate in a different session are invisible to it. So is the context my teammates' AIs have. Each of us, and each of our AIs, is operating with a fraction of what we collectively know."

Three sharing personas

The session-amnesia problem is the floor. How each tier wants their AIs to work together is what differs. Sharing happens through a Slack channel the tier already uses — channel membership is the scope-enforcement primitive. No new tool adoption.

Engineering sharing

IC engineer · tech lead · SRE

Problem statement

"When an engineering decision lands in Slack — an architectural call, a pattern we agreed on, a fix one of us found — I want that to land in every team member's Claude automatically, so the next time anyone's AI weighs in on related code, it has the same opinions our team already formed."

What mem-fusion + Slack-connector solves

Decisions made in eng Slack channels propagate into every team member's local Claude context. New hires onboard their AI by pulling the channel's accumulated history. Bug-report → fix loops close end-to-end through the channel without manual relay.

Top use cases
  • Shared architectural opinions across all team members' AI assistants
  • Bug-fix corpus: a fix one engineer ships becomes context everyone's AI has
  • Onboarding-by-osmosis: new hire's AI inherits team patterns from day one
  • Code-review coherence: reviewer + author AIs converge on same standards

Product sharing

IC PM · group PM · designer · researcher

Problem statement

"When a product decision gets made — in a Slack thread, a team-channel post, a 1:1 recap — I want that decision available to every PM's Claude, so we stop having parallel exploratory conversations about the same question and junior PMs inherit context they weren't in the room for."

What mem-fusion + Slack-connector solves

Weekly updates, partner-conversation summaries, strategic framings stored as memory flow through the product team's Slack channel. Every PM's AI sees what every other PM's AI has been told. Roadmap context, stakeholder maps, and decision history surface automatically across the team.

Top use cases
  • Shared strategic context: research findings + partner conversations + decisions
  • Decision-history retrieval: "why did we pick X over Y?" answers consistently
  • Cross-functional convergence: PM + design + research AIs share understanding
  • PM-transition continuity: incoming PM's AI inherits departing PM's context

Executive sharing

C-suite · senior leadership · chief of staff

Problem statement

"When a strategic call gets made — in a 1:1 DM, an exec channel, a leadership meeting — I want my Claude, my peer execs' Claudes, and our chiefs of staff's Claudes to all see it at exec-tier scope, so when any of us asks our AI about a strategic topic, we get a coherent answer instead of a fragment."

What mem-fusion + Slack-connector solves

Leadership-meeting decisions, cross-exec alignments, board-prep context, capital and commercial calls flow through a private exec Slack channel. Every exec AI shares the same strategic state. Chiefs of staff act on behalf of the exec with appropriately-scoped AI access.

Top use cases
  • Cross-exec strategic context: "what's our position on X" answers consistently
  • Decision propagation: calls made in 1:1s become visible to relevant peers
  • Quarterly board-prep continuity: "what did we promise last quarter?"
  • Chief-of-staff delegation: proxy AI access with appropriate confidentiality scope

How they want their AI to work together — the nuances

Dimension Engineering Product Executive
What gets shared code patterns, bug-fixes, design choices, debugging lessons strategic decisions, research, partner conversations, roadmap framings cross-functional decisions, board context, capital + commercial calls
Memory volume high (code patterns numerous) medium (decisions less frequent) low (high-importance fewer)
Acceptable sharing latency seconds hours – days minutes – hours
Slack channel shape team channel (medium audience) product-org channel (medium audience) private exec channel (small audience; CoS may join)
Confidentiality posture team-wide OK within the channel team + sometimes wider exec-tier only; legal-privileged awareness
AI-to-AI shape code-reviewer ↔ code-author PM ↔ designer ↔ research exec ↔ peer exec ↔ chief of staff
Sharing scope mechanism Slack channel membership. Same primitive for all three tiers — different channels, different scopes. No new privacy primitive required.
Why Slack works as the substrate for all three tiers: Each tier already uses Slack for the conversations where the relevant decisions get made. Adding mem-fusion is zero-new-tool adoption. Slack's channel-membership model IS the scope-enforcement model — engineering channel for eng memory, product channel for product memory, private exec channel for exec memory. Per-user connector.json entries map a channel-id to a tier scope. The same architectural primitive ({id, type: "slack", channel}) serves all three personas.
